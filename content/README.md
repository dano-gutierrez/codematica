# Canonical content

Knowledge Markdown is schema-validated curriculum; directory guides belong outside `knowledge/` so they do not become unsourced lessons. JSON under exercises, paths and sources supplies practice, curriculum scope and provenance.

## Creative computing

Original finite paper models separate visual prediction, authoritative state, sampled geometry and trigger lifetime. Units and source scope are explicit. No engine, downloaded asset or tutorial scene is executed.

## Business models

Original ownership arithmetic declares issued shares, financing assumptions and omitted rights. The source-linked SAFE illustration is educational reading, not a chosen financing contract or a legal/investment outcome.

See [Selected Evidence Review](../docs/features/selected-evidence-review.md) and each content directory guide.

## Frontend system design interviews

`knowledge/frontend/system-design-*.md` and matching guided labs provide the full frontend interview guide and six attributed whiteboard exercises. The path starts with architecture decisions and three illustrated examples, followed by the rehearsal schedule and rubric. Technical references and unverified candidate reports remain distinct; authored practice additions are labeled. See [the feature contract](../docs/features/frontend-system-design-interviews.md).

## Forward deployed engineering

`knowledge/fde/` and `exercises/fde/` hold the original customer-delivery curriculum. The role path, eleven primary sources and 32-card review feed use existing local-first contracts. Labs use synthetic data and self-reported evidence; reported customer outcomes are attributed separately from original practice. See [the feature contract](../docs/features/forward-deployed-engineer-path.md).
