## What You Will Do

Read, recognize, type, and use beginner Japanese for **Identity And Demonstratives**. This is original N5-aligned preparation, not an official JLPT word list or score guarantee. Writing tasks support learning; the official JLPT N5 exam itself measures language knowledge, reading, and listening.

## Core Grammar

### Noun + です

**Pattern:** `Noun + です`  
Make a polite statement identifying or describing something.

- 私は学生です。
- I am a student.

### Noun + は

**Pattern:** `Noun + は`  
Mark the topic that the rest of the sentence comments on.

- 今日は月曜日です。
- Today is Monday.

### Noun + も

**Pattern:** `Noun + も`  
Add that the same statement is true of another topic.

- 私も学生です。
- I am also a student.

### Sentence + か

**Pattern:** `Sentence + か`  
Turn a polite statement into a neutral question.

- 学生ですか。
- Are you a student?

### Noun + の + Noun

**Pattern:** `Noun + の + Noun`  
Connect nouns for possession, affiliation, or description.

- これは私の本です。
- This is my book.

### これ・それ・あれ

**Pattern:** `これ・それ・あれ`  
Point to a thing near the speaker, listener, or neither person.

- それは何ですか。
- What is that?
