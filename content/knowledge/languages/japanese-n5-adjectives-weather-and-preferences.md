## What You Will Do

Read, recognize, type, and use beginner Japanese for **Adjectives, Weather, And Preferences**. This is original N5-aligned preparation, not an official JLPT word list or score guarantee. Writing tasks support learning; the official JLPT N5 exam itself measures language knowledge, reading, and listening.

## Core Grammar

### Verb stem + ましょう

**Pattern:** `Verb stem + ましょう`  
Invite someone to do an action together.

- 一緒に帰りましょう。
- Let us go home together.

### Verb stem + ませんか

**Pattern:** `Verb stem + ませんか`  
Invite someone politely while leaving room to decline.

- 映画を見ませんか。
- Would you like to watch a movie?

### Verb stem + たいです

**Pattern:** `Verb stem + たいです`  
Express what the speaker wants to do.

- 日本へ行きたいです。
- I want to go to Japan.

### Noun + がほしいです

**Pattern:** `Noun + がほしいです`  
Express that the speaker wants a thing.

- 新しい自転車がほしいです。
- I want a new bicycle.

### い-adjective + Noun/です

**Pattern:** `い-adjective + Noun/です`  
Describe a noun or make a polite statement with an i-adjective.

- 今日は暑いです。
- It is hot today.

### な-adjective + な + Noun

**Pattern:** `な-adjective + な + Noun`  
Use a na-adjective before a noun or with です as a predicate.

- 静かな町です。
- It is a quiet town.
