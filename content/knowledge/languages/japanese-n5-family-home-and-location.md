## What You Will Do

Read, recognize, type, and use beginner Japanese for **Family, Home, And Location**. This is original N5-aligned preparation, not an official JLPT word list or score guarantee. Writing tasks support learning; the official JLPT N5 exam itself measures language knowledge, reading, and listening.

## Core Grammar

### Time + に

**Pattern:** `Time + に`  
Mark a specific time when an action occurs.

- 七時に起きます。
- I get up at seven.

### Noun + から・まで

**Pattern:** `Noun + から・まで`  
Mark the starting and ending point of time or place.

- 九時から五時まで働きます。
- I work from nine to five.

### Noun + と + Noun

**Pattern:** `Noun + と + Noun`  
Join nouns in a complete list meaning and.

- パンと卵を買います。
- I buy bread and eggs.

### Noun + や + Noun

**Pattern:** `Noun + や + Noun`  
Give representative examples from a larger list.

- 机の上に本やペンがあります。
- There are books, pens, and such on the desk.

### Verb stem + ます

**Pattern:** `Verb stem + ます`  
Express a habitual or future action politely.

- 毎日日本語を勉強します。
- I study Japanese every day.

### Verb stem + ません

**Pattern:** `Verb stem + ません`  
Express that a present or future action does not happen.

- 今日は働きません。
- I will not work today.

## Practice Loop

1. Read each word aloud without romaji.
2. Complete the mixed recognition quiz.
3. Use the open-answer activity to convert romaji or Pencil handwriting into Japanese.
4. Return later through Japanese review for cumulative flashcards.
