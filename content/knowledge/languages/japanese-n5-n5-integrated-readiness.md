## What You Will Do

Read, recognize, type, and use beginner Japanese for **Integrated N5 Readiness**. This is original N5-aligned preparation, not an official JLPT word list or score guarantee. Writing tasks support learning; the official JLPT N5 exam itself measures language knowledge, reading, and listening.

## Core Grammar

### negative stem + なくてもいいです

**Pattern:** `negative stem + なくてもいいです`  
Say that an action is not required.

- 明日は来なくてもいいです。
- You do not have to come tomorrow.

### plain past/negative + ほうがいいです

**Pattern:** `plain past/negative + ほうがいいです`  
Give gentle advice about a preferable action.

- 早く寝たほうがいいです。
- You should go to bed early.

### Noun + だけ

**Pattern:** `Noun + だけ`  
Limit a statement to only the marked item or amount.

- 水だけ飲みます。
- I drink only water.

### Noun + でも

**Pattern:** `Noun + でも`  
Offer one representative option in a casual suggestion.

- お茶でも飲みませんか。
- Would you like tea or something?

### Sentence + ね

**Pattern:** `Sentence + ね`  
Invite agreement or acknowledge shared information.

- 今日はいい天気ですね。
- The weather is nice, isn't it?

### Sentence + よ

**Pattern:** `Sentence + よ`  
Present information the listener may not know.

- この電車は東京へ行きますよ。
- This train goes to Tokyo, you know.

2. Complete the mixed recognition quiz.

4. Return later through Japanese review for cumulative flashcards.
