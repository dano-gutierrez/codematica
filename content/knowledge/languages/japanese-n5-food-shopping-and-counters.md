## What You Will Do

Read, recognize, type, and use beginner Japanese for **Food, Shopping, And Counters**. This is original N5-aligned preparation, not an official JLPT word list or score guarantee. Writing tasks support learning; the official JLPT N5 exam itself measures language knowledge, reading, and listening.

## Core Grammar

### Person + と

**Pattern:** `Person + と`  
Mark the person who participates in an action with someone.

- 友達と話します。
- I talk with a friend.

### Tool or vehicle + で

**Pattern:** `Tool or vehicle + で`  
Mark the means, instrument, language, or transport used.

- 電車で行きます。
- I go by train.

### Noun + をください

**Pattern:** `Noun + をください`  
Politely ask for an item in a shop or restaurant.

- 水をください。
- Please give me water.

### て-form + ください

**Pattern:** `て-form + ください`  
Politely request that another person perform an action.

- ここに名前を書いてください。
- Please write your name here.

### て-form + もいいです

**Pattern:** `て-form + もいいです`  
Ask for or grant permission to do something.

- 写真を撮ってもいいですか。
- May I take a photo?

### て-form + はいけません

**Pattern:** `て-form + はいけません`  
State that an action is prohibited.

- ここで食べてはいけません。
- You must not eat here.

4. Return later through Japanese review for cumulative flashcards.
