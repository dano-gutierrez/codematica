## What You Will Do

Read, recognize, type, and use beginner Japanese for **Routines, Verbs, And Particles**. This is original N5-aligned preparation, not an official JLPT word list or score guarantee. Writing tasks support learning; the official JLPT N5 exam itself measures language knowledge, reading, and listening.

## Core Grammar

### Verb stem + ました

**Pattern:** `Verb stem + ました`  
Express a completed past action politely.

- 昨日映画を見ました。
- I watched a movie yesterday.

### Verb stem + ませんでした

**Pattern:** `Verb stem + ませんでした`  
Express that an action did not happen in the past.

- 朝ご飯を食べませんでした。
- I did not eat breakfast.

### Noun + を + Verb

**Pattern:** `Noun + を + Verb`  
Mark the direct object affected by an action.

- 水を飲みます。
- I drink water.

### Place + で + Verb

**Pattern:** `Place + で + Verb`  
Mark the place where an action happens.

- 図書館で勉強します。
- I study at the library.

### Place + へ + movement verb

**Pattern:** `Place + へ + movement verb`  
Mark the direction toward which someone moves.

- 学校へ行きます。
- I go toward school.

### Place + に + movement verb

**Pattern:** `Place + に + movement verb`  
Mark a concrete destination or arrival point.

- 東京に来ました。
- I came to Tokyo.

4. Return later through Japanese review for cumulative flashcards.
